Comprehensive Orthodontic Treatments at Northeast Orthodontics

Every smile is different, which is why selecting the right treatment matters. Northeast Orthodontics offers several orthodontic solutions designed to help children, teens, and adults improve their smiles.

Traditional Braces

Traditional metal braces remain one of the most reliable options for correcting dental alignment problems. They use brackets and wires to apply controlled pressure, gradually moving teeth into their desired positions.

Braces can help correct:

  • Crooked and crowded teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Overbites and underbites
  • Crossbites and other bite concerns
  • Complex orthodontic alignment problems

With modern orthodontic techniques, braces are designed to provide effective and predictable results for patients with different dental needs.

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Patients looking for a more discreet way to straighten their teeth may consider Invisalign clear aligners. These custom-made, removable trays gradually move teeth into better positions without traditional metal brackets and wires.

Invisalign can be a convenient option for eligible teenagers and adults who want flexibility during orthodontic treatment.

Some advantages include:

  • Clear and discreet appearance
  • Removable trays for eating and drinking
  • Easier brushing and flossing
  • Customised treatment planning
  • Comfortable everyday use

An orthodontic consultation can help determine whether Invisalign is suitable for your individual smile goals.

What to Expect During Your First Orthodontic Consultation

If you have never visited an orthodontist before, knowing what to expect can make your first appointment more comfortable.

Your initial consultation generally involves several important steps:

1. Dental Evaluation: The orthodontist examines your teeth, bite, and jaw alignment to understand your orthodontic concerns.

2. Discussing Your Goals: You can explain what you would like to improve, whether it involves crowded teeth, gaps, or bite problems.

3. Treatment Recommendations: Based on your assessment, the orthodontist discusses suitable options, such as braces or Invisalign.

4. Understanding Your Treatment Plan: You can learn about the expected treatment process, appointments, and estimated duration.

5. Financial Information: Discuss available payment arrangements and treatment costs before making your decision.

How to Maintain Your Smile During Orthodontic Treatment

Following good oral hygiene habits is important throughout your orthodontic journey. Taking proper care of your teeth and orthodontic appliances can help support a healthy treatment experience.

Consider these helpful tips:

  • Brush your teeth regularly using a soft-bristled toothbrush.
  • Floss daily to remove food particles and plaque.
  • Follow your orthodontist's instructions for cleaning braces or aligners.
  • Avoid hard and sticky foods that may damage braces.
  • Wear Invisalign aligners according to your orthodontist's recommendations.
  • Attend scheduled orthodontic appointments.
  • Continue visiting your general dentist for routine dental checkups.

Following professional advice can help protect your teeth and support your final orthodontic results.
